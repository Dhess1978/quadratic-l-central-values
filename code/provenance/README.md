# Provenance: the scripts as they were run

These are the original scripts that produced the numbers of study V, copied without change. They are a record, not a package: they contain the paths of the computers on which they ran (`/home/claude/…` on the analysis computer, the folders of computer 2 for PARI/GP), read intermediate files that are not part of the publication (the zeros of the L-functions, caches of ζ values), and their comments are in Czech, as written. Every number of the article is collected in `data/results/study_v_results.json`; `code/independent_check_v.py` recomputes the main ones from the published central values without using any of these scripts.

| folder | what |
|---|---|
| `values_and_zeros/` | PARI/GP (cypari2) generators of the central values and zeros: `ks_nuly_values_V_B10_C100_R10_D100.py` (values of every fundamental discriminant up to 10⁷ and of D100), `ks_nuly_values_D100b_D100c.py`, `ks_nuly_zeros_D100b_D100c_values_E10.py` (zeros of D100b and D100c, PARI values of E10; SHA-256 `2006a966…`, see `data/manifests/`), `ks_nuly_zeros_S7.py` (zeros of S7 and of S9L at 10⁹ and the PARI values of the check sample S9K — the same file defines all three families; SHA-256 `1b53fe0e…`); the own smoothed functional equation at 10¹⁰ (`afe_L.py`, `afe_L2.py`) and at 10⁹ (`afe_L3.py`, weights expanded around the centres of segments of |D| of width 10⁴; the list of discriminants from the sieve `s9l_D.py`; the check against the 2,000 PARI values `s9k_kontrola.py`) and the reading of the zeros of E from study III (`e_nuly.py`); the checks against mpmath, against the independent functional equation and against PARI (`mp_check_*.py`, `kontrola_L*.py`, `lcheck.py`, `e10_kontrola.py`, `pari_vzorek.py`); the sieve and completeness checks (`fund_disc.py`, `uplnost_D100bc.py`, `nacti.py`) |
| `moments_cfkrs/` | contour integrals of the CFKRS polynomials Q_k (`cfkrs.py`, `qk_chunk.py`, `qk_run.py`, `run_q*.py`, `q_v2_merge.py`, `q4_check.py`), moments by band (`momenty*.py`), the moments at 10⁸ and the scaling of the blocks (`h0_h1.py`), consecutive blocks (`bloky.py`), the comparison with the tables of Alderson and Rubinstein (`ar_tab.py`, `srovnani_ar.py`) |
| `distribution_and_zeros/` | distribution of ln L and the hybrid model (`rozdeleni.py`, `zlomtrend.py`), the term X^{3/4} in the subfamily D = 8d after Diaconu and Whitehead (`q3sub.py`, `b34.py`), USp(2N) tables (`usp_dpp*.py`, `usp_jacobi.py`), the central value and the first zero (`vazba_x1.py`, `analyzy_3477.py`, `h1_alfa.py`, `h2_vaha*.py`, `h3_hust.py`, `h4_split.py`, `nef.py`, `hough.py`), the splitting conjecture (`buikeating.py`), the logarithmic scaling of consecutive blocks (`bloklog.py`) |
| `weighted_density/` | the weighted one-level density after Fazzari, eq. (4.8) (`fazzari.py`), a second implementation for the classes of D mod 8 derived from the first (`fazzari_tridy.py`) — both contain the error in one Γ factor corrected in Section 4.6 of the article, and the null model by shuffling L between discriminants (`vaha_nul.py`) |
| `weighted_density_correction/` | the correction before publication (Section 4.6): the independent implementation (`nez_kusy.py`, `nez_porovnani.py`; the same code in `code/weighted_density_independent.py`), the test of the faulty factor in the pieces of the first implementation (`oprava_q4.py`), the corrected numbers (`oprava_vysledky.py`), the explicit formula on single L-functions (`ef_jeden.py`) and on C100 with the tail by the rule (`ef.py`, `ef_ocas.py`); the family S7 computed after the correction: check of the blocks against the manifest (`s7_sha.py`), the weighted density and the verdicts (`s7_analyza.py`), the descriptive test on consecutive and random pieces (`s7_kusy.py`), the prime side computed before the zeros arrived (`ef_prvocisla.py`, checked against the stored parts of C100 to 1e-14), the explicit formula with that store (`ef_a.py`) and the tail by the rule for any family (`ef_ocas_a.py`); the family S9L at 10⁹: check of the blocks against the manifest (`s9l_sha.py`), the weighted density and the verdict (`s9l_analyza.py`, frozen before the zeros arrived; its SHA-256 is recorded in the rules) and the descriptive moments against CFKRS (`s9l_momenty.py`) |
| `families_1e8_1e10/` | the analyses of D100b, D100c and E at 10⁸ and 10¹⁰, including the replication at 10⁸ and the fits of Figure 7 (`lu_analyza.py`, `lu8_analyza.py`, `lu9_analyza.py`) |
| `ford_zaharescu/` | the calibration of the appendix on the zeros of ζ (`fz.py`, `fz2.py`) |

The rules recorded before each step are in `data/rules/` (Czech, with dates); the step numbers 34.76–34.85b in the comments refer to them.
